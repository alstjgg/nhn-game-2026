# discovery — e0

## Seam friction

- **`ReportGuidance` has two plausible owners in the same wave.**
  contract-engine-composer §3 makes `ComposerDeps.reportGuidance` a
  `ReportGuidance`, but e1 owns `src/shared/report-guidance.ts` and e0 must not
  write it (`file_globs` are exclusive). e0's skeleton therefore has to declare
  the shape locally or type the field structurally. `tests/scaffold/skeleton.test.ts`
  deliberately asserts only that `ComposerDeps extends { reportGuidance: unknown }`
  so the two units cannot collide on it; **e5 is the unit that reconciles them**,
  and if e1 exports a canonical `ReportGuidance`, the composer should re-point at
  it rather than keep a second copy.

## Reference ambiguities

- **contract-engine-composer §9 is a list of open items, not a run-loop
  surface.** The unit's read-scope calls §9 "run-loop manager surface"; what §9
  actually fixes is *where* it lives (`src/runloop/`, isomorphic, no DOM) and
  that its `meta` shape "settles when it is built". The concrete `RunLoop` /
  `MetaStore` / `MetaState` names the tests pin are therefore e0's, derived from
  PRD decisions 13 and 15 plus `data/runs/_schema/meta-state.schema.json`
  (which is the authority on the persisted field names — `pack_slug`,
  `run_count`, `exposure_clock_reached`, `carried_blocks`, `report_archive`).
  Same for the transport surface: §3 covers the composer, and §11 of
  contract-calls is where the wire actually lives. Both sections were opened
  beyond the sliced read-scope.

- **The `<nn>` in `b-r<run>-<channel><nn>` is a *minimum* width, not a cap.**
  §2.0's examples (`b-r1-n02`, `b-r1-f01`) are two-digit and 1-based; nothing
  says what a 100th sentence looks like. The tests pin `padStart(2, '0')`
  semantics — index 100 mints `b-r1-f100` rather than truncating — because
  truncation would collide two ids and archive highlighting is keyed on them.
