# discovery — e3 (beat & round driver)

> Seeded by the TEST phase. The BUILD phase appends the §2 decisions of
> `.claude/super/units/e3/spec.md` that the docs did not state.

## Read-scope misses (TEST phase)

- **The unit's read scope named `spec.md`, `docs/spec-engine.md` §3.1/§4.x and the
  frozen 우는다리 pack — but not the type sources the tests must import.** Writing
  fixtures against `Timeline`/`Gates`/`Characters`/`Temperament` required
  `src/shared/datapack.ts`, and the three view shapes required
  `src/engine/index.ts` (e0) plus `Stance`/`PresentNpc` from
  `src/shared/contracts.ts`. All three were opened section-only, read-only, and
  are named by `design.md` §"Existing patterns reused" — the miss is in the
  decomposer's scope list, not in the design.

## Under-specified seams the tests had to pin

- **`beatAt(...)` (spec A6) is not on `design.md`'s exported surface.** The tests
  express it as a lookup over `buildSchedule`'s output (`tests/engine/beat/harness.ts`),
  so no production helper is required. If BUILD prefers to export one, the tests
  do not object — they simply do not depend on it.
- **`design.md` D-D lists `narration → (report)` but does not say what `phase()`
  returns on a round-last beat.** The tests pin: `applyBeatEffects()` leaves
  `phase() === 'report'` on a round-last beat and `'narration'` otherwise;
  `roundView()` is legal exactly in `'report'`; `recordLines()` is legal in both.
  The `report` step is appended when that phase is entered, which is what makes
  A7's "immediately after that round's last beat" an assertion on `steps()`.
- **A3's "call2 index" is not observable on the state log**, because the driver
  makes no LLM call (spec D10) and the step log is a separate array. The tests
  prove it in three legs instead: the narration step does not exist before
  `applyBeatEffects()` and does exist after; the full effect chain is already on
  the state log at that moment; and `beatView()` — the Call-2 payload — throws
  until the effects have landed and adds no state ops once they have.
