# Oral-history interview — protocol (slice S10)

**Decision (2026-08-05): no blind upfront questionnaire.** A generic
concept-phase question set — the kind that can be written *before* seeing the
evidence — only re-asks what OH-1/OH-2 already record, is blind to the 835 mined
atoms and to what deliverable #4's narrative actually needs, and (if handed to a
teammate mid-recall) contaminates the independent account we most want. Interview
questions are worth asking only when **derived from evidence**. Two evidence-driven
rounds replace the upfront one:

## Round 1 — divergence & thin-section follow-ups
- **Trigger:** after OH-3 (윤석's *independent* timeline) lands.
- Compare OH-1/OH-2 (민서) against OH-3 (윤석). Ask only where the accounts
  **disagree**, or where one is thin on a moment the other detailed.
- Questions are generated *from* the three accounts, not before them. Example
  shapes (to be filled once OH-3 exists): did the membrane feel like a founding
  agreement or a negotiated compromise? who first proposed DDAY, and at what
  moment? why exactly did each demoed concept lose?

## Round 2 — narrative-gap resolution
- **Trigger:** during / after Phase 2 theme induction.
- Theme induction surfaces which oral-only claims are **load-bearing** for the
  deliverable, and which themes have holes only a human can fill. Ask exactly
  those, and nothing else.
- Highest-value round: driven by what #4 needs, which is unknown until themes
  exist.

## Both rounds
- Answer **separately first** (independent recall is the signal), then a short
  **joint pass** on divergences + genuinely two-person moments (e.g. the
  in-game-AI negotiation, which neither person can reconstruct alone).
- Append each written answer as an OH-n verbatim block in `oral-history.md`.
